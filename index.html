Key takeaway

Who's Afraid of Chinese Models?
Stratechery (Ben Thompson) · August 18, 2026
Key takeaway
Thompson reads China's open-model posture as a textbook “commoditize your complements” play: give the models away, because the value accrues to whoever leads in the physical layer — robotics and manufacturing — that widely available models will unlock. The non-obvious move is recognizing that a company giving something away for free is telling you exactly where it thinks the profit pool actually is. Apply this principle to your own competitive analysis: when a rival makes part of their stack free or open, stop asking whether they can afford it and start asking which adjacent layer they intend to own, because that layer is the one you should be defending.
