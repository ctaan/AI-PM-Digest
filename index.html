PM Thought Leaders

Must read
Who’s Afraid of Chinese Models?
Stratechery (Ben Thompson) · July 20, 2026
Key takeaway
Thompson argues that open-weight Chinese models like Kimi K3 aren’t actually cheap — tokens are not a commodity because different models burn very different amounts of reasoning tokens to reach the same answer, so the real unit is “intelligence” and its true cost is a function of serving efficiency. The mental model shift for PMs: stop comparing models on headline per-token price and start comparing them on total cost to complete your actual task at your quality bar. Apply this principle to build-vs-buy and model-selection calls — run your real workflow end-to-end on each candidate and measure cost-to-outcome, because the “cheaper” model often loses once you count the tokens it needs to get there.

Claude Code for Product Managers
Every (Marcus Moretti) · Updated July 20, 2026
Key takeaway
Moretti runs an entire product solo by making Claude Code the hub of his PM work: he personally writes only the roadmap, and Claude drafts every PRD and ticket, collapsing work that used to span ten apps into a single chat thread. The concrete workflow to build this week: pick one PM ritual — ticket writing, PRD drafting, weekly metrics — and move it into one agent-driven thread where you supply direction and the agent produces the artifact. Internalize his framing that “the conversation is the work,” and that as writing gets cheap, your leverage shifts to deciding what to build and reviewing sharply rather than producing every document by hand.
