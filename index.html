This CPO regrets that product management exists | Tom Verrilli (CPO of Whatnot)
Lenny’s Newsletter · August 2, 2026
Key takeaway
Verrilli — previously CPO at Twitch and product growth lead at Twitter, now running product at Whatnot through $8B+ in GMV — built his team on the premise that “we regret that product management exists,” and argues that fewer, more senior PMs doing real IC work beat any ratio-driven org structure. The career implication is sharp: seniority is being redefined as depth of hands-on ownership rather than span of control, which inverts the promotion path most PMs were taught to climb. Internalise this when planning your own next step — the defensible version of senior is the one where you personally still ship, write the strategy, and touch the product, not the one where you coordinate people who do.

OpenAI Codex lead on the new shape of product work | Andrew Ambrosino
Lenny’s Newsletter · June 28, 2026
Key takeaway
Ambrosino, who leads the Codex desktop app at OpenAI, makes the case that as software gets radically cheaper to build, taste becomes the scarce input — and lays out what that does to the shape of product work. The mental model shift: when the cost of producing an option collapses, the bottleneck moves from “can we build it” to “which of these twenty things deserves to exist,” and that judgment is not something you can delegate to a model. The concrete practice to adopt this week is to spend the time you save on execution generating more candidate solutions before committing to one, then defend your pick on taste and strategy rather than feasibility. For career development, this is the clearest signal yet that curation and judgment are the durable PM skills to compound.
