Applied AI Workflows for PMs

Must read
Fable’s judgement
Simon Willison · July 3, 2026
Key takeaway
Willison distills a sharp operating rule for agentic work: use your judgement to route implementation to an appropriately lower-power model running in a subagent, while keeping design, review, data synthesis, and anything judgment-heavy on the top-tier main model. The mental model shift: model selection is a per-task routing decision, not a single global default — cheap models for mechanical work, expensive models for reasoning. Apply this to your own AI workflows this week by explicitly tiering the steps of a repeatable task, and you cut cost sharply without touching the quality of the parts that actually need judgment.
