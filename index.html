Anthropic says it will watermark text generated by its AI models
TechCrunch · August 11, 2026
Key takeaway
Anthropic will watermark model-generated text and files to satisfy the EU AI Act’s Transparency Code, with every model released after August 2 carrying the marking automatically. The mental model shift for PMs: provenance is becoming a default property of AI output rather than a compliance add-on you bolt on later, which means any feature that generates or edits customer-facing text now inherits a regulatory surface. Apply this principle by asking, for each AI feature in your roadmap, whether you can answer “which model produced this, and can we prove it” — if the answer is no, that is a design gap, not a legal one.

Key takeaway
Verrilli — CPO at Whatnot, previously CPO at Twitch and product growth lead at Twitter — argues that fewer, more senior PMs doing real individual-contributor work outperform any ratio-driven org structure, at a company that has become the fastest-growing US marketplace in history with over $8B in GMV. The career implication is direct: seniority is being redefined as depth of hands-on ownership rather than span of coordination, so the PM who personally holds a hard problem end-to-end is worth more than the one who runs a wider process. Internalise this when you choose what to take on next — pick the scope you can actually do the work inside, not the scope with the most stakeholders attached to it.

Stratechery (Ben Thompson) · August 3, 2026
Key takeaway
Thompson steps back from the quarter-to-quarter AI coverage to look at what the technology does to people rather than to markets — the kind of piece that is easy to skip and disproportionately useful when you are the one deciding what a product asks of its users. 5 Trends That Defined AI Engineering at World’s Fair 2026
Latent Space · July 2026
Key takeaway
The standout trend is “harness engineering” — Lilian Weng’s argument that the system surrounding the model (workflow management, context, permissions, evaluation, persistent state, continuous improvement) now matters as much as the model itself, alongside the rise of the forward-deployed engineer inside enterprises. This is the single most useful mental model on today’s list because it relocates the product surface: your differentiation is the harness, and the harness is almost entirely product decisions rather than model decisions. The concrete move this week is to write down the harness for one AI feature you own — what context it gets, what it is permitted to do, how you evaluate it, what it remembers — and you will find the gaps immediately.
