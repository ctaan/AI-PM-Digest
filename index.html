Lenny's Newsletter · May 24, 2026
Key takeaway
Dan Shipper (Every) makes the counterintuitive case that more automation tends to create more work and more roles for humans, not fewer — because cheaper execution expands what's worth doing and surfaces new demand. The framing PMs should adopt: automating a workflow rarely shrinks the job; it shifts effort toward judgment, orchestration, and the higher-order problems that were previously out of reach. Applied AI Workflows for PMs

Must read
How top PMs increase their leverage with AI | Colin Matthews
Lenny's Newsletter · June 30, 2026
Key takeaway
Colin Matthews, who has trained 30,000+ PMs, lays out three "ladders of leverage" — personal (AI drafts text → makes artifacts → completes whole to-do items), product (web prototypes → codebase prototypes → agent-shipped PRs), and systems (repeatable, self-checking workflows) — noting executives now expect AI to complete entire tasks, not just assist. The concrete workflow to build this week: connect your LLM to your real tools via MCP (Amplitude, PostHog, Notion, Figma), delegate a full task like a retention analysis or launch review with "cite your sources" so you can validate, then lock the winning prompt in as a reusable skill. Internalize the framing that the right rung depends on the task, not on always climbing higher — the goal is matching AI leverage to the work in front of you, not maximizing sophistication for its own sake.
